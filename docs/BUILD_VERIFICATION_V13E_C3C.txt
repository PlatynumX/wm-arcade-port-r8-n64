=======================================================

Purpose
-------
C3c fixes the historical DRONE.ASM script generator after the real Termux
preflight reached `reachable script label drn_seek not found in active
DRONE.ASM body`.

Root cause
----------
C3b sliced to the final `.file "drone.asm"` body before macro expansion.  The
range generator expanded the complete historical translation unit first.  A
macro defined outside the final body can therefore remain live when invoked
inside it; the two generators were looking at different effective source.

Correction
----------
- Expand the complete DRONE.ASM translation unit first.
- Select the final production `.file "drone.asm"` body only after expansion.
- Keep final-body selection so archived earlier DRONE bodies do not become live.
- Add a regression fixture with a macro defined before `.file` that generates
  `#drn_seek` when invoked in the active body.
- Preserve all C3b symbol/inline/WL fixes, C3a include/equate fixes, and C2e
  Sports replacement assets.
